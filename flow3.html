12 Potential FCS Over FBS Upsets for the 2026 College Football Season

Stan Becton
Jan 21, 2026 · 10 minute read
Share article

After four FCS teams stunned FBS opponents last season, what could be in store for 2026? Here are the games that could join the list next and should be on your radar.

Week 1: Austin Peay at Vanderbilt · South Dakota State at Northwestern · Tarleton State at Bowling Green · Rhode Island at Temple

We’ll start with two Power Five teams that should be on high alert. Vanderbilt doesn’t have Diego Pavia anymore. Austin Peay still has Chris Parson. Parson might be the best quarterback on the field come Week 1, and that’s always a necessity for an FBS upset. Austin Peay also upset an FBS team just one year ago.

Northwestern plays perennial FCS power South Dakota State. Northwestern head coach David Braun is all-too-familiar with the Jackrabbits after prior stints in his coaching career at Northern Iowa and North Dakota State. While Northwestern is coming off a 7-6 season, South Dakota State will likely be a preseason top-five FCS team — the Jackrabbits have a real chance.

Two more highly regarded FCS teams entering 2026 are Rhode Island and Tarleton State. Both will face FBS teams coming off seasons with losing records. Tarleton State upset Army last year and Rhode Island most recently landed an FBS upset in 2021. It would be no surprise if either squad added another win to the all-time list.

Week 2: Montana State at Nevada · Illinois State at Northern Illinois · Wofford at Kent State · Sacred Heart at UMass

The two teams from last year’s FCS national championship game play FBS teams that weren’t very good last year in Week 2. Both Nevada and Northern Illinois went 3-9 last year, while Montana State and Illinois State are primed for upsets in the two hardest FCS conferences.
